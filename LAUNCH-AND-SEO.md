# Marketing website launch & SEO checklist

See README.md for setup and required owner approvals. This document supersedes the earlier marketing launch notes.

## Technical foundation delivered

- Server-rendered titles and meta descriptions for each marketing route.
- Canonical base: `https://www.smartsmbhub.com`.
- Branded 1200 × 630 social image and site icon.
- Semantic headings and one H1 per marketing page.
- Organization and WebSite schema on home; truthful Service schema on service pages.
- Crawlable service and concept links; no invented local addresses, review stars, or performance claims.
- Sitemap with indexable public marketing routes. Paused storefront, draft policies, private account areas, and API paths are omitted.
- Preview deployments noindex; private areas noindex; paused products noindex; draft policies noindex.
- Existing service URLs retained. Unknown paths render a genuine HTTP 404.

## Before production

- Confirm www/non-www preference and matching Vercel redirects.
- Verify the chosen market and service wording. Do not create thin city pages or fictitious office locations.
- Approve the legal drafts; then adjust their indexing only if appropriate.
- Confirm all forms and configured booking links with real, authorized tests.
- Check mobile navigation, FAQs, service links, plan-interest preselection, and keyboard access on the Vercel Preview.
- Run PageSpeed/Lighthouse against the deployed site. No speed score or Core Web Vitals result has been claimed from local layout tests.
- Review current Search Console data and preserve valuable legacy URLs or map redirects for any future changes.

## After launch

- Verify Search Console ownership and submit `/sitemap.xml`.
- Inspect homepage and service-page URLs for indexability and canonical selection.
- Track meaningful outcomes: qualified audit requests, inquiry sources, bookings, and actual client acquisition—not merely clicks.
- If adding analytics, implement privacy-appropriate configuration and update the privacy notice first.
- Build content around real customer questions and actual delivered work.
- Replace concept examples with verified projects as available; retain disclosures until that happens.

Website redesign and metadata provide a foundation, not a guarantee of search rankings, traffic, leads, or revenue.
